# Content production order

Current stage: **Horror — first 100 distinct published feature films**.

The owner clarified “رعب”, not Arabic films. No Arabic-language-only restriction is added. Arabic subtitles remain preferred.

## Stage 1 — Horror

Prioritize supernatural horror: sorcery, witchcraft, jinn/djinn, possession, demons, ghosts and haunted houses. Crime, murder investigations and ordinary thrillers alone do not qualify as horror. Require story/source evidence of horror classification.

Published target: 100. Currently published horror: 0. Discovery candidates never count as published.

Each film needs >=90 minutes, year >=2005, verified commercial streaming rights, real compatible media, real cover art with reuse permission, bilingual original synopsis, details, and tested playback. Only licensed Arabic/English subtitle tracks may appear.

## Later stages

After the horror batch, process each remaining main genre as its own 100-film production batch. Do not scatter acquisition effort across every genre before finishing stage 1. The taxonomy remains fully visible in the UI even while content is absent.

Series remains a separate empty section until movie priorities are complete or the owner changes them.

## Tracking

Track discovered, needs review, rights verified, media tested and published independently. Record a blocking reason per review title. Shared genre labels never inflate the distinct movie total.
