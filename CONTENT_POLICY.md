# Content Policy

Cineyah excludes piracy, unclear rights, pornography and sexually explicit works, trailers or clips presented as films, noncommercial licenses, broken sources, fake quality labels, and misleading metadata. Every public asset requires an auditable item-level license record. Uncertainty means hidden **Needs Review**.

## Movie publication gate

A public movie must satisfy every rule below:

- Release year 2005 or later for the current acquisition phase.
- Feature length of at least 90 minutes. Short films, clips, trailers, and videos below 90 minutes never enter the public movie catalog.
- Direct MP4, HLS, or DASH playback that may legally be used inside Cineyah. A branded YouTube embed is not accepted for public playback.
- Explicit commercial-use permission and any required attribution.
- Verified working media and honest rendition labels; no fake HD.
- Clear Movie classification. Ambiguous Movie/Series records go to Needs Review.
- A useful, non-infringing bilingual synopsis or Needs Review.
- Conservative mainstream content-safety review.

TV episodes are exempt from the 90-minute minimum and retain their natural runtime. Runtime is stored internally in minutes but rendered as localized hours/minutes (`1 ساعة و30 دقيقة` / `1h 30m`) wherever viewers see it.

## Subtitles and derivatives

- The public player shows only Arabic, English, and Off.
- Arabic is enabled automatically when a licensed Arabic track is present.
- Extra source-language tracks are not exposed in the Cineyah interface.
- A subtitle may be imported or translated only when its own license and the film license allow the required commercial reuse and derivative work.
- Unknown, scraped, fan-made, or otherwise unlicensed subtitle files are prohibited.
- Absence of a legal Arabic track is disclosed; one is never fabricated or borrowed from a piracy source.

## License allowlist

Potentially acceptable after item-level verification: CC BY, CC BY-SA, CC0, and demonstrably public-domain material. NonCommercial licenses are rejected for an ad-supported service. NoDerivatives material cannot receive a translated or modified subtitle track. Download is shown only when the license and source terms specifically allow the required redistribution.
